My current interests can be summarized into four areas:

- ⚖️ **Socio-legal Studies**

- 🏛️ **Governance**

I'm particularly interested in how institutions, rules, evidence, technologies, and social structures interact — and how some of these relationships can be translated into systems that are **retrievable, structured, verifiable, and reusable**.

---

## 🌊 What I'm Building

### [SeaFlow](https://github.com/HuDeyi-66/SeaFlow)

**SeaFlow** is my long-term legal AI and evidence infrastructure project.

It explores how heterogeneous sources can be transformed into reliable, traceable, and machine-usable evidence while preserving provenance, citation, and evidentiary boundaries.

Current principles:

> **Evidence First · Traceable Sources · Small Core · Extensible Skills**

SeaFlow started from legal research and evidence retrieval, and is gradually evolving into a modular evidence infrastructure.

---

### 📘 [ShanHai / 海珊](https://github.com/HuDeyi-66/Shan_Hai_Code_Skill)

**ShanHai** is the text-evidence retrieval skill of SeaFlow.

It focuses on:

- source retrieval
- exact text extraction
- structural recognition
- native text citation
- ambiguity handling
- explicit refusal when evidence is insufficient

Its core principle is:

> **Find the source before answering the question.**

---

### 📊 [LuoHai / 海珞](https://github.com/HuDeyi-66/Luo_Hai_Tables_Skill)

**LuoHai** focuses on structured and tabular evidence.

It is designed to work with heterogeneous spreadsheets, reports, tables, and structured datasets while preserving their source boundaries and traceability.

Its core principle is:

> **Organizing evidence ≠ creating evidence.**
