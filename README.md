## 🌊 What I'm Building

### SeaFlow

**SeaFlow** is my long-term legal AI project.

It explores how legal sources can be transformed into reliable, traceable, and machine-usable evidence while preserving provenance, citation, and evidentiary boundaries.

Current principles:

> **Evidence First · Traceable Sources · Small Core · Extensible Skills**

SeaFlow began with maritime law and is gradually developing toward a broader legal evidence infrastructure.

---

### 📘 ShanHai / 海珊

**ShanHai** is the legal-text evidence skill of SeaFlow.

It focuses on:

- legal source retrieval
- exact text extraction
- legal structure recognition
- native text citation
- ambiguity handling
- explicit refusal when evidence is insufficient

> **Find the source before answering the question.**

---

### 📊 LuoHai / 海珞

**LuoHai** focuses on structured and tabular evidence.

It is designed to work with heterogeneous spreadsheets, tables, reports, and structured datasets while preserving their original evidentiary boundaries.

> **Organizing evidence ≠ creating evidence.**
